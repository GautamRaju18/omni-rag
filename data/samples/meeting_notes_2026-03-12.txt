Project Aurora - Technical Review
Date: 2026-03-12
Attendees: Dr. Priya Raman, Marco Lindqvist, Aiko Tanaka, Samuel Okafor

1. High-temperature failure of AUR-C103
Aiko presented the post-mortem for AUR-C103. The cell failed at cycle 412 at 45 C with
14% pouch swelling. Gas analysis points to electrolyte decomposition of the E-5 baseline,
not a manufacturing defect. Samuel confirmed the seal integrity tests on the same batch passed.

2. Decision: move high-temperature testing to electrolyte E-7
The team agreed to stop 45 C testing with E-5 and to qualify E-7 instead. Early E-7 cells
(AUR-C105 and AUR-C106) are above 89% retention after 600 cycles at 45 C.
Marco Lindqvist owns the E-7 qualification and will report swelling data per checkpoint.

3. Milestone M2 risk
Priya noted that milestone M2 (45 C stability demonstrated, due 2026-05-30) is at risk if
E-7 swelling exceeds the 5% limit. A fallback is E-5 + FEC, which is at 83.1% after 800
cycles on AUR-C104.

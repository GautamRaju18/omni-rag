Aurora S1 Sodium-Ion Cell

The Aurora S1 is a 50 Ah pouch cell for stationary energy storage, built on the Project Aurora chemistry: a Prussian white cathode and hard carbon anode.

Why sodium-ion?

  • No lithium, nickel or cobalt
    • Lower raw-material cost
    • Less supply-chain risk
  • Can be shipped at 0 V for safer transport

Availability

Engineering samples are expected after milestone M3 (pilot line 100-cell batch).
